# SkillBridge 🔧

### Connecting People with Trusted Local Skilled Workers

**SkillBridge** is a mobile application designed to connect people with reliable local skilled workers for everyday services. Whether someone needs an electrician, plumber, carpenter, mechanic, painter, or another skilled professional, SkillBridge provides a simple way to discover, evaluate, and connect with suitable service providers.

The idea behind SkillBridge is to solve a common everyday problem: **finding trustworthy skilled workers quickly and confidently** while also giving skilled workers a digital platform to showcase their abilities and reach more customers.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Finding a reliable skilled worker often depends on personal recommendations, local contacts, or informal networks. This can make the process time-consuming and uncertain.

At the same time, many skilled workers have limited opportunities to promote their services and reach new customers.

**SkillBridge bridges this gap by bringing customers and skilled workers together on one platform.**

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 User Features

* Create and manage a personal account
* Search for skilled workers
* Browse workers based on their services
* View detailed worker profiles
* Check ratings and customer reviews
* View worker experience and skills
* Send service requests
* Connect with suitable workers
* Manage personal profile and requests

### 🔧 Skilled Worker Features

* Create a professional worker profile
* Add skills and offered services
* Showcase experience
* Receive service requests
* Manage customer requests
* Build reputation through ratings and reviews
* Reach potential customers in their local area

### ⭐ Ratings & Reviews

Customers can rate workers after receiving a service. Reviews help future customers make better decisions and allow reliable workers to build credibility.

### 📍 Local Service Discovery

SkillBridge focuses on connecting customers with workers who provide services in their required area, making it easier to find locally available professionals.

### 🔐 Authentication & Security

Firebase Authentication is used to provide secure user authentication and account management.

## 🌟 Why SkillBridge?

SkillBridge is more than a directory of workers. It focuses on building **trust between customers and service providers** through worker profiles, ratings, reviews, service information, and local discovery.

The platform aims to make the process of finding a skilled worker:

**Faster → Easier → More Transparent → More Trustworthy**
